# Major Additions:
## PLAYER PARAMETERS
 - You can now tweak your player parameters in the pre-match interface (Preparation) -> selecting which player to tweak parameters for. Parameters include AI Performance Level, Solid Garbage Height, and Spice Handicap Level.

## GACHATRIS SOLID GARBAGE
 - The first Gachatris game to ever feature board-restricting Solid Garbage! This feature applies to Gachatris: a block that doesn't clear as a whole. If a line is full of Solid Garbage, it will not clear up nor count. Solid Garbage does not affect whether a Perfect Clear is registered. In other words, a Perfect Clear cannot be registered if there is a block on the board except Solid Garbage blocks.

## RPG Mode (Alpha):
 - This mode is in Alpha stage, meaning that there are bugs present in the mode. If you discover bugs in this mode, report them to the developer.
 - A player can only pick 3 UNIQUE characters as cards in their deck; they can use the same character as their opponent. All 3 characters in their deck add up HP and Mana depending on the attributes of each of them.
 - Each character has a unique skill or so, with some remarkable features, buffs, and debuffs contained; enough mana will be used for every skill use. Mana costs vary on a card.
 - The goal of a player is to force their opponent's HP down to 0. Every top-out or pile-out costs them a fraction of their HP.
